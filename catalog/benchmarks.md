# Catalog: benchmarks

Benchmarks and benchmark research related to this repo's artifacts. These live
elsewhere under their own licenses — **link, don't fork**.

## benchmarks.law

Independent legal-AI benchmark publication. Canonical home for the benchmark
projects below.

- Site: <https://benchmarks.law/>

## editors_trial

Thirteen DOCX editors put through round-trip fidelity testing: 99 checks,
8 reports, 10 hostile packages.

- Code: <https://github.com/arthrod/editors_trial>
- Report: <https://benchmarks.law/editors-trial/>

## neurotic_docx_bench

DOCX converters and redline engines scored against Word-as-oracle: 6,427
documents, 3,502 pairs, fixtures published as a Hugging Face dataset.

- Code: <https://github.com/jandira-tech/neurotic_docx_bench>
- Report: <https://benchmarks.law/neurotic-docx-bench/>

## Bench 03 / Bench 04 (cite only)
