Eviction notice check board

Check an eviction notice's facts against jurisdiction rules-as-data. Every requirement gets PASS / FAIL / UNVERIFIED with the rule cited. Anything not positively attested stays UNVERIFIED — the checker never guesses.

The bundled jurisdiction rulesets are editable example data with US defaults (CA, TX, NY, WA, OH, FL, GA, AZ, IL, OR plus a fictional city overlay). Notice law varies by state, city, and lease, and changes often — verify every cited rule against current law. Not legal advice.
