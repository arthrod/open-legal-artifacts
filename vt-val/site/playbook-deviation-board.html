Playbook deviation board

A clause board classified by pure code against playbook-as-data: same playbook + contract always yields the same board. Every classified row quotes the clause span that drove the decision, numbers in standards must match verbatim, and fallback tiers route to approvers (tier ≥ escalation threshold goes to the escalation owner). Every deviation carries a deterministic redline back to the preferred standard and a decision-trace panel (rule chain, score vs thresholds, char span, routing). The playbook below is parsed in your browser by the core's own dependency-free YAML parser. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact.

Worked example (fictional): Verdant Robotics mutual-NDA playbook v3 × a Northwind draft — 8 clauses, including a perpetual-term deal-breaker and an uncapped-confidentiality carve-out.
