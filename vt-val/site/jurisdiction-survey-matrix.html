Jurisdiction survey matrix

Jurisdictions × legal questions in one table — every populated cell carries a source citation, a link to the underlying law, and a verification date; stale cells are flagged, and unverified cells are never guessed. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact.

Load your own survey below (paste or file) — everything renders client-side; no data leaves the machine. Worked example (fictional): breach-notification rules, 7 jurisdictions × 4 questions, 180-day re-verification policy.
