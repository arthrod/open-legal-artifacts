Entity compliance calendar

Filing deadlines across every entity in the corporate structure — computed from rules-as-data obligations (annual, biennial, event-driven) and each entity's formation anchor, with delinquency flags and CSV/ICS export. The rule base, not the model, is the product: every obligation carries its (fictional) statutory cite.

Worked example (fictional): Northwind Robotics group, 5 entities × 5 obligations, filing history included — missed filings surface as the earliest unfiled period, not last year's.
