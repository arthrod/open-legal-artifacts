Sell-side Q&A log

A diligence Q&A log whose core cross-examines the answers: questions carry buyer, category, and answer history (draft/sent); deterministic heuristics detect contradictions between answers — numbers by unit, terms by antonym pairs — each flagged with verbatim evidence quotes; and a disclosure-readiness score is computed from the log itself. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact.

Worked example (fictional): Project Copperfield, three buyers, eight questions — including a pending draft that quietly changes the EBITDA story.
