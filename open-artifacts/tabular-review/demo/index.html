Tabular review

A documents × questions matrix where the core verifies, never answers: a cell counts as answered ONLY when its answer carries a quote that actually locates in the document (exact or normalized; fuzzy anchors but is flagged for review). Everything else is UNVERIFIED with a machine reason — not_answered, missing_quote, quote_not_verified — and anchors survive CSV/Markdown export. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact.

Worked example (fictional): 3 agreements × 5 questions — 15 cells, including one deliberately unanswered and one quote that does not locate (the California governing-law cell).
