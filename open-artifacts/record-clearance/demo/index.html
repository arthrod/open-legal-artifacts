Record clearance tracker

Relief types live in editable per-jurisdiction rules-as-data. The engine turns a client record into a prerequisites checklist (met / not met / unknown — never guessed), computes earliest and last filing dates with plain date math, tracks matters already filed, and raises referral flags for counsel-dependent relief. Full-tier example jurisdictions: CA, IL, NY — plus a guidance tier (TX): a generic screen with a mandatory referral instead of a bare error. Every item carries filing guidance (forms, steps, fee-waiver note) and an authority link ([verify]-tagged — illustrative).
