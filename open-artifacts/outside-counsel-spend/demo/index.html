Outside counsel spend

A spend audit whose core is the findings ledger: every hourly entry checked against the negotiated rate card for that firm × matter × date, budget variance net of write-offs, and over-threshold write-offs flagged — each finding citing the entry, the rule, and the dollar impact. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed dashboard artifact.

Worked example (fictional): Verdant Robotics, two firms, three matters, Aug–Oct 2026. Integer-cents arithmetic, no hidden clocks.
