Matter board

A matter board where change detection is the core: diff two snapshots and every field change is flagged with a timestamp, a severity, and the source of the timestamp. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact — a board that ages is a board that lies; this one shows exactly what moved.

Worked example (fictional): week-44 → week-45 practice-management snapshots, 4–5 matters, 14-day deadline window.
