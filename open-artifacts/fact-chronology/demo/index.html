Fact chronology

A timeline where every fact carries its evidence: dates normalized at three precisions, a source anchor (document + verbatim quote + pinpoint) on each entry, conflicts flagged inside topics (date mismatches, divergent accounts), and gaps surfaced — while undated entries stay visible instead of being dropped. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact.

Worked example (fictional): Delgado v. Summit Ridge Hospitality — 10 facts across 5 documents, including a termination date the complaint and the letter disagree about by one day.
