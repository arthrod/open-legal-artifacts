Renewal calendar

Last day to give notice on each contract — from its terms, normalized honestly: explicit dates schedule exactly, ambiguous expressions like "within 60 days of quarter end" are scheduled conservatively with a flag and both readings, and unresolvable expressions are listed for review, never guessed into a date. ICS export included.

Worked example (fictional): Northwind Robotics contract portfolio, lead times as rules-as-data (auto-renewal 90d, termination 30d, price-adjustment 120d, default 60d).
