Regulatory horizon scan

Feed of regulatory items in, deduped + classified + impact-ranked scan out — scored by a transparent, versioned rubric you can audit, re-weight, and diff. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact; the scoring is never a black box.

Every point of every score carries a factor, a rule, and a reason. Comment windows and ANPR/RFI nuance are first-class; non-primary sources are flagged and capped; undated items are kept on the timeline, never dropped.
