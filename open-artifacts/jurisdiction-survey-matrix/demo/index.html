open-legal-artifacts.exe

Jurisdiction survey matrix

Jurisdictions × legal questions in one grid — every populated cell carries a source citation, a link to the underlying law, and a verification date. Stale cells are flagged for re-verification and unverified cells are never guessed: an answer without citation, http(s) source link, and a parseable verification date cannot render as verified.

Worked example (all fictional): employee non-compete enforceability, 51 jurisdictions × 5 questions = 255 cells with an honest verified/stale/unverified spread, 180-day re-verification policy. All client-side; no data leaves the machine.
