Jurisdiction survey matrix

Jurisdictions × legal questions in one table — every populated cell carries a source citation and a verification date, stale cells are flagged, and unverified cells are never guessed. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact.

Worked example (fictional): breach-notification individual-notice rules, 7 jurisdictions × 4 questions, 180-day re-verification policy.
