Cite check

Every quotation in a legal document verified against its cited source — no LLM in the loop: exact / normalized / fuzzy / not_found classification, verbatim anchors with char offsets, and pinpoint validation (p. / § / ¶) against the segment where the quote actually lives. Open reimplementation of Anthropic's closed artifact.

Worked example (fictional): appellant's opening brief with 10 quotes — including one paraphrase that drifts, one silently altered digit caught by the number guard, one omission without an ellipsis, one correct quote cited to the wrong page, and one source not in the record.
